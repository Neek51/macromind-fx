     3. News spikes — CPI/FOMC ke around fake breakout mein fasna

     7. Information overload — 30 tabs open hain, decision freeze ho gaya

     1. Process repeat karna — same checklist, every trade, no exceptions
     2. Small risk, high selectivity — kam trades, better trades
     3. Regime samajhna — trending market mein trend follow, ranging mein mean reversion
     4. Apne stats jaanna — mujhe pata hai main news ke din loss karta hoon, London open mein best perform karta hoon
     5. Losses ko small rakhna — win rate se zyada important risk control hai
